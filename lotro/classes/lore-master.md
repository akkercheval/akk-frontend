The Lore-master is a ranged class that bends the forces of nature against its
foes — dealing elemental damage, debuffing enemies, healing allies, and
commanding animal companions. It's one of my two main raiding classes and is
**highly sought after in raids** for the utility it brings.

## Is the Lore-master Right for You?

**This is not a beginner class.** There's a lot going on — elemental skills,
pets, debuffs, and group utility all at once. That said, it can be a lot of fun
for a seasoned gamer coming to LotRO for the first time, if you don't mind a
steep learning curve.

A signature feature of the class is the ability to **summon pet companions.**
Each pet type has a different specialisation — see the
[pet guide below](#lore-master-pets) for the full list.

## Trait Trees

The Lore-master has three trait lines, and all three can be selected as your
primary trait tree. When you first open the class trait panel, before you've
committed to a line, it looks like this:

![The Lore-master class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/lore-master/lore-master-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### Keeper of Animals — Pet Damage & Utility (Blue)

The Blue line, **Keeper of Animals**, focuses on damage and utility through your
pets. It's a fun trait line for soloing.

![The Lore-master's Keeper of Animals (blue) trait tree, focused on pet damage and utility](/images/LotRO/classes/lore-master/lore-master-class-traits-blue.png)

### Master of Nature's Fury — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **Master of Nature's Fury**, focuses on damage. What makes
Lore-master damage unique is that you deal **multiple kinds of damage — fire,
lightning, frost, and light — depending on the skill.**

The trade-off: in this line you **lose access to a permanent pet companion.**
Instead, you can briefly summon one for a short time. The payoff is damage
that's currently competitive at end game. My kinship has several Lore-master
players, and we often bring two — one for damage and one for debuffs.

![The Lore-master's Master of Nature's Fury (red) trait tree, focused on elemental damage](/images/LotRO/classes/lore-master/lore-master-class-traits-red.png)

### The Ancient Master — Utility (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Ancient Master**, is a utility build and is **highly
sought after for its debuffs in group content.** You can debuff enemies, heal
your allies, and crowd-control your enemies — all from one line.

![The Lore-master's Ancient Master (yellow) trait tree, focused on utility and debuffs](/images/LotRO/classes/lore-master/lore-master-class-traits-yellow.png)

## Lore-master Pets

Lore-masters can summon pet companions, but only **one pet at a time.** In the
Blue and Yellow lines, your pet stays with you until you dismiss it or it's
defeated in combat. (As noted above, the Red line trades the permanent pet for
a brief, temporary summon.)

Here are the pet types and their primary roles:

| Pet | Primary role |
|---|---|
| **Bog-guardian** | Ranged damage, with a chance to stun its target — opening a fellowship manoeuvre |
| **Raven** | Debuffs enemies and buffs allies |
| **Saber-cat** | Area-of-effect (multi-target) melee damage |
| **Lynx** | Melee damage, and can attack from stealth |
| **Bear** | Tanking and enemy debuffs (the tanking ability is disabled in instances) |
| **Nature Spirit** | Heals and buffs allies |
| **Eagle** | Damages enemies; can sacrifice itself if you're defeated, though this doesn't trigger reliably |
