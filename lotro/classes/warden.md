The Warden is a mostly-melee class built around a system found nowhere else in
the game: **Gambits.** It is widely regarded as the most challenging class in
LotRO — but in skilled hands it can do some genuinely remarkable things.

[Jump to the Gambit system &rarr;](#the-gambit-system)

## Is the Warden Right for You?

Let me be direct: **this is not an easy class.** In fact, I'd call it the most
challenging class in the game. Playing a Warden well generally takes dedication
and a good memory.

That said, a skilled Warden can do some pretty crazy things. In the past,
Wardens have soloed instances that are absolutely not designed to be completed
alone. Reaching that level of mastery earns you real respect.

**I recommend this class for someone who:**

- likes a challenge,
- is willing to dedicate themselves to mastering it, and
- enjoys pushing the boundaries of what looks possible.

You don't see many people raiding with a Warden, but I believe that's down to
the learning curve rather than any lack of viability in group settings.

## The Gambit System

The single most important thing to understand about the Warden is **Gambits.**

![The Warden's Gambit panel](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-gambit-panel.png)

### Gambit Builders

You build Gambits using three basic **builder skills.** Each one damages your
target and adds a single Gambit icon to the Gambit panel:

| Builder skill | Background | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick Toss** | Red | One spear Gambit |
| **Behind the Shield** | Green | One shield Gambit |
| **Warden's Aim** | Yellow | One fist Gambit |

![The Warden's three basic Gambit builder skills](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-gambit-builder-skills.png)

### Building and Spending Gambits

Gambits build **from left to right** in the panel. A new Warden has **three
Gambit slots**, and over time unlocks two more for a total of **five.**

As you level up, more Gambit skills unlock — each one triggered by a specific
sequence of builders. The screenshot below shows the Warden's Gambit skills
panel. Note that it does **not** show every possible Gambit combination skill:

![The Warden's Gambit skills panel](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-gambit-skills-panel.png)

Because there are so many combinations, most skilled Wardens I know keep a
**"cheat sheet"** of their most commonly used Gambits close at hand — don't feel
you have to memorize everything at once.

## Trait Trees

The Warden has three trait lines: **two can be selected as your primary trait
tree**, and the third is a support line. When you first open the class trait
panel, before you've committed to a line, it looks like this:

![The Warden class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### Determination — Tank (Blue)

The Blue line, **Determination**, focuses on tanking. It can successfully tank a
single boss and has impressive survivability in skilled hands.

![The Warden's Determination (blue) trait tree, focused on tanking](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-class-traits-blue.png)

### Recklessness — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **Recklessness**, focuses on single-target damage with bleeds.

![The Warden's Recklessness (red) trait tree, focused on single-target bleed damage](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-class-traits-red.png)

### Assailment — Support (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **Assailment**, **cannot be selected as your primary trait
line.** It contains many good traits that increase your damage and apply enemy
debuffs.

![The Warden's Assailment (yellow) trait tree, which boosts damage and applies debuffs](/images/LotRO/classes/warden/warden-class-traits-yellow.png)
