The Brawler is a pure melee class that fights with its fists — punching enemies
down close up rather than relying on weapons at range. It wears Heavy armour
and makes a sturdy, satisfying front-line fighter.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Purchased class (not available by default) |
| **Armour** | Heavy |
| **Primary stat** | Might |
| **Roles** | Tank · Damage (plus a support buff line) |
| **Playstyle** | Pure melee — close-quarters, fist-fighting |

## Is the Brawler Right for You?

This is a purchased class, but unlike some of the other bought classes it is
**fairly easy to learn.** If you enjoy close combat and want a melee class
that's approachable, the Brawler is a great fit.

## Trait Trees

The Brawler works a little differently from most classes. It has **two
selectable trait trees** — one for tanking, one for damage — plus a third line
that you can't choose as your primary specialisation but that still shapes how
you play. When you first open the class trait panel, before you've committed to
a line, it looks like this:

![The Brawler class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/brawler/brawler-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Fulcrum — Tank (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Fulcrum**, focuses on tanking: holding the enemy's
attention and absorbing punishment so your group stays safe.

![The Brawler's Fulcrum (blue) trait tree, focused on tanking](/images/LotRO/classes/brawler/brawler-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Maelstrom — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Maelstrom**, focuses on dealing damage.

![The Brawler's Maelstrom (red) trait tree, focused on damage](/images/LotRO/classes/brawler/brawler-class-traits-red.png)

### The Fundament — Support (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Fundament**, is different from the other two: you
**cannot select it as your primary trait tree.** Instead, it provides valuable
buffs to you and your allies. Some of these buffs are highly sought after in
raid groups, which makes the Brawler a welcome addition to a raid roster.

![The Brawler's Fundament (yellow) trait tree, which provides group buffs](/images/LotRO/classes/brawler/brawler-class-traits-yellow.png)
