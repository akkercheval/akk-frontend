The Champion is a pure melee class — wading into the thick of the fight and
cutting down everything in reach. It's free, easy to learn, and a lot of fun
for anyone who enjoys close combat.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Free class |
| **Armour** | Heavy |
| **Primary stat** | Might |
| **Roles** | Damage (single-target and area-of-effect) |
| **Playstyle** | Pure melee — close combat |

## Is the Champion Right for You?

**Yes — this is one I recommend for beginners.** It's easy to learn and fun to
play, which makes it a great way to get comfortable with melee combat.

The one catch: this poor class is **not currently highly favoured in raids,**
which is a shame. The good news is that the developers are always tweaking class
balance, so there's always hope its damage will improve in a future update.

## Trait Trees

The Champion has three trait lines, and all three can be selected as your
primary trait tree. When you first open the class trait panel, before you've
committed to a line, it looks like this:

![The Champion class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/champion/champion-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Martial Champion — Defence (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Martial Champion**, focuses on defence and tanking. It is
**never selected as a group tank**, but it does hold some good defensive traits
that are worth spending points on at higher levels.

![The Champion's Martial Champion (blue) trait tree, focused on defence](/images/LotRO/classes/champion/champion-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Berserker — Single-Target Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Berserker**, focuses on single-target melee damage. It's a
good line for levelling a new character and does decent damage, though it
currently lags behind other classes.

![The Champion's Berserker (red) trait tree, focused on single-target damage](/images/LotRO/classes/champion/champion-class-traits-red.png)

### The Deadly Storm — Area-of-Effect Damage (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Deadly Storm**, focuses on area-of-effect (multi-target)
attacks — we call this the **"blender"** line for Champions. It's a lot of fun
to play and the most likely to find a spot in groups, where it's great for
quickly clearing out adds (a boss's allies).

![The Champion's Deadly Storm (yellow) trait tree, focused on area-of-effect damage](/images/LotRO/classes/champion/champion-class-traits-yellow.png)
