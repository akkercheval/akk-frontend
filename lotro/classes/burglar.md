The Burglar is a melee class built around damage and debuffs — slipping in
close to carve down enemies while weakening them for the rest of the group.
Burglars are highly sought after for the debuffs they bring, which makes them a
valued support pick in group content.

## Is the Burglar Right for You?

The Burglar is a close-combat class that shines in a support role, so it's a
great fit if you enjoy fighting up close and helping your group with debuffs
and control.

That said, it is **not a good first class for a new player** — there's a lot of
nuance to playing it well. But once you're comfortable in the game, it can be a
lot of fun.

## Trait Trees

The Burglar has three trait lines, and **all three can be selected as your
primary trait tree.** When you first open the class trait panel, before you've
committed to a line, it looks like this:

![The Burglar class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/burglar/burglar-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Gambler — Damage (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Gambler**, focuses on damage and leans heavily on critical
strikes to unlock more powerful hits. It's a fun spec, but **I don't recommend
it while you're still learning the class** — it's easier to appreciate once the
fundamentals are second nature.

![The Burglar's Gambler (blue) trait tree, focused on critical-strike damage](/images/LotRO/classes/burglar/burglar-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Quiet Knife — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Quiet Knife**, also focuses on damage. It's great for
soloing: its stealth buffs let you sneak around large packs of enemies out on
the landscape. **If you're new to the class, this is the trait line I'd start
with.**

![The Burglar's Quiet Knife (red) trait tree, focused on damage and stealth](/images/LotRO/classes/burglar/burglar-class-traits-red.png)

### The Mischief-Maker — Support (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Mischief-Maker**, focuses on debuffing enemies and crowd
control. This is the line that makes the Burglar such a strong group pick.

![The Burglar's Mischief-Maker (yellow) trait tree, focused on debuffs and crowd control](/images/LotRO/classes/burglar/burglar-class-traits-yellow.png)
