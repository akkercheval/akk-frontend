The Hunter is a ranged damage class — picking enemies off with a bow from a
distance. It's **Melda's class**, and one of the three classes I've played the
most and the longest, so this one is close to my heart.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Free class |
| **Armour** | Medium |
| **Primary stat** | Agility |
| **Roles** | Ranged damage (with crowd-control utility) |
| **Playstyle** | Ranged — bow attacks from mid to long range |

## Is the Hunter Right for You?

The Hunter is easy to learn and a **good starting point for new players.** Most
people have a Hunter tucked away somewhere, though it's worth being honest:
relatively few spots go to Hunters in raids right now.

For a new player, I recommend starting in the **Bowmaster** line for its
simplicity.

## Trait Trees

The Hunter has three trait lines, and all three can be selected as your primary
trait tree. When you first open the class trait panel, before you've committed
to a line, it looks like this:

![The Hunter class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/hunter/hunter-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### Huntsman — Mobility Damage (Blue)

The Blue line, **Huntsman**, is a damage line focused on mid-range attacks and
mobility. It also includes some buffs to melee damage, though long-time Hunters
tend to largely ignore those.

![The Hunter's Huntsman (blue) trait tree, focused on mid-range damage and mobility](/images/LotRO/classes/hunter/hunter-class-traits-blue.png)

### Bowmaster — Long-Range Damage (Red)

The Red line, **Bowmaster**, is a stationary, long-range line. It does good
damage and gives you additional area-of-effect (multi-target) attacks. This is
the line I recommend for new players.

![The Hunter's Bowmaster (red) trait tree, focused on stationary long-range damage](/images/LotRO/classes/hunter/hunter-class-traits-red.png)

### Trapper of Foes — Crowd Control (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **Trapper of Foes**, focuses on traps for crowd control. It
was reworked fairly recently and generally isn't chosen as a primary trait
line, though it has some good utility traits.

![The Hunter's Trapper of Foes (yellow) trait tree, focused on traps and crowd control](/images/LotRO/classes/hunter/hunter-class-traits-yellow.png)

## How Hunters Actually Trait

In practice, the community is split between the Red and Blue lines. Most of us
run a **hybrid of the two**, choosing one or the other as the primary depending
on the situation. If you're just starting out, don't worry about the hybrid
dance yet — begin with Bowmaster and branch out as you get comfortable.
