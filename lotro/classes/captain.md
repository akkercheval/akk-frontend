The Captain is a melee combat class that leads from the front — fighting up
close while bolstering everyone around them. In groups, the Captain's main job
is to buff allies, and it does that so well that it's a welcome addition to
almost any roster. It's a class that rewards players who enjoy close combat and
juggling several things at once.

## Is the Captain Right for You?

The Captain is best for players who like close combat and multi-tasking.
There's a lot to keep track of, so **I don't recommend it for beginners** — but
it has some genuinely interesting features that make it worth the effort.

A couple of things set the Captain apart:

- **A summoned follower.** Captains can call a follower that buffs you and your
  allies and can also deal damage.
- **Buffing is the real job.** In groups, the Captain's primary role is buffing
  allies — even though there isn't a single trait line dedicated purely to that
  purpose. The buffs are spread across the kit, which is part of what makes the
  class interesting to play.

## Trait Trees

The Captain has three trait lines. When you first open the class trait panel,
before you've committed to a line, it looks like this:

![The Captain class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/captain/captain-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### Hands of Healing — Healing (Blue)

The Blue line, **Hands of Healing**, is dedicated to healing your allies. It is
**not currently favoured for group healing**, so you'll see it less often in
that role.

![The Captain's Hands of Healing (blue) trait tree, focused on healing](/images/LotRO/classes/captain/captain-class-traits-blue.png)

### Lead the Charge — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **Lead the Charge**, is focused on damage. **This is the line I
recommend starting with when learning the Captain.** It is not a fast-killing
class, but it's good if you have a friend to play with. In groups, Captains
typically pair this line with traits from the Yellow line for buffs.

![The Captain's Lead the Charge (red) trait tree, focused on damage](/images/LotRO/classes/captain/captain-class-traits-red.png)

### Leader of Men — Tank & Buffs (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **Leader of Men**, is dedicated to both tanking and buffs.
Captains can tank single targets fairly well, though they are **not currently
favoured as main raid tanks.** This line carries many traits that are highly
valuable in group settings, which is why group Captains lean on it so heavily.

![The Captain's Leader of Men (yellow) trait tree, focused on tanking and buffs](/images/LotRO/classes/captain/captain-class-traits-yellow.png)
