The Beorning is a shape-changer who fights in close, swapping between a human
form and a mighty bear form. It is one of Middle-earth's most distinctive
classes — and one of its most flexible, able to tank, heal, or deal damage
depending on how you train it.

## Is the Beorning Right for You?

The Beorning is a melee class — it fights its enemies up close rather than at
range. If you enjoy being in the thick of the fight, it is a lot of fun to
play.

That said, **I don't recommend the Beorning for true beginners.** It is a
purchased class rather than one you start with, and it carries several advanced
mechanics — most notably managing its two forms — that are easier to pick up
once you already know your way around the game.

For players who are comfortable with the basics, the Beorning rewards you well:

- A **solid place in end-game content**, playable as both a healer and a tank.
- **Good damage for soloing** and small-group content.
- The flexibility to fill whichever role your group needs.

## Trait Trees

Like most classes, the Beorning has three distinct trait trees. Each one is
tied to a color and leans toward a particular role. When you first open the
class trait panel — before you've committed to a line — it looks like this:

![The Beorning class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/beorning/beorning-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Hide — Tank (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Hide**, focuses on tanking: soaking up damage and holding
your enemies' attention so your group stays safe.

![The Beorning's Hide (blue) trait tree, focused on tanking](/images/LotRO/classes/beorning/beorning-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Claw — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Claw**, focuses on dealing damage.
