The Minstrel sings its allies back to health and its foes into the ground — the
game's original healer, and still one of its best. It's **my primary class**
and one of the three I've played since the very beginning of the game, so I
have a real soft spot for it.

## Is the Minstrel Right for You?

The Minstrel is a free class and **pretty good for beginners.** Its primary role
in groups is healing, but it's more versatile than that: it can do decent
damage — particularly multi-target damage — and it provides good group buffs.
That mix of healing, damage, and support makes it a friendly, flexible class to
learn on.

## Trait Trees

The Minstrel has **two selectable trait trees** — one for healing, one for
damage — plus a support line that both specs lean on heavily. When you first
open the class trait panel, before you've committed to a line, it looks like
this:

![The Minstrel class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/minstrel/minstrel-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Watcher of Resolve — Healing (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Watcher of Resolve**, focuses on healing. Minstrels were
the first healer in the game and still excel at group healing.

![The Minstrel's Watcher of Resolve (blue) trait tree, focused on healing](/images/LotRO/classes/minstrel/minstrel-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Warrior-Skald — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Warrior-Skald**, focuses on damage — especially burst and
area-of-effect (multi-target) damage. It's a good line for levelling and
soloing, and it's currently decent in groups, though that hasn't always been
the case.

![The Minstrel's Warrior-Skald (red) trait tree, focused on burst and area-of-effect damage](/images/LotRO/classes/minstrel/minstrel-class-traits-red.png)

### The Protector of Song — Support (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Protector of Song**, **cannot be selected as your primary
trait line.** It contains many very useful buffs for you and your allies — so
useful that both blue and red "minnies" trait deep into this line as well.

![The Minstrel's Protector of Song (yellow) trait tree, which provides group buffs](/images/LotRO/classes/minstrel/minstrel-class-traits-yellow.png)
