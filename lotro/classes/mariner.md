The Mariner is the newest class in LotRO, released in **2023**. It's a melee
support class built around a unique balance mechanic, and it always has a place
in raids thanks to the support it brings.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Premium class (not free) |
| **Armour** | Medium |
| **Primary stat** | Agility |
| **Roles** | Support · Melee damage · Debuffs & healing |
| **Playstyle** | Melee support with a dynamic balance mechanic |

## Is the Mariner Right for You?

The Mariner is **not an easy class to play**, so I don't recommend it for new
players. For experienced gamers, though, its mechanics are genuinely
interesting and make for a fun challenge. And because it's such a reliable
support class, there's always a spot for one in raids.

## The Balance Panel

The Mariner's signature mechanic is its **balance panel.** Using your skills
shifts the balance back and forth:

- **Defensive skills** shift the balance to the **left — the blue side, labeled
  Aft.**
- **Offensive skills** shift the balance to the **right — the red side, labeled
  Fore.**

Managing where your balance sits is central to playing the Mariner well, which
is a big part of what makes the class such an interesting challenge.

![The Mariner's balance panel, which shifts left or right as you use defensive or offensive skills](/images/LotRO/classes/mariner/mariner-balance-panel.png)

## Trait Trees

The Mariner has three trait lines, and all three can be selected as your primary
trait tree. When you first open the class trait panel, before you've committed
to a line, it looks like this:

![The Mariner class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/mariner/mariner-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Shanty-caller — Buffs (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Shanty-caller**, focuses on buffing your allies.

![The Mariner's Shanty-caller (blue) trait tree, focused on buffing allies](/images/LotRO/classes/mariner/mariner-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Duellist — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Duellist**, focuses on melee damage.

![The Mariner's Duellist (red) trait tree, focused on melee damage](/images/LotRO/classes/mariner/mariner-class-traits-red.png)

### The Rover — Debuffs & Healing (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Rover**, focuses on debuffing enemies and healing.
