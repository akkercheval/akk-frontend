The Rune-keeper channels power through runes and rune-stones, excelling at
**single-target healing and single-target damage.** Both roles find it easy to
get into groups, which makes the Rune-keeper a flexible, in-demand pick.

## Is the Rune-keeper Right for You?

I wouldn't recommend the Rune-keeper for a true beginner, but it's **fairly easy
to learn, versatile, and fun to play.** If you want a class that can heal or
deal damage and slot comfortably into groups either way, it's a great choice.

One of its signature tools is the **rune-stone**: a placeable object that either
damages or heals, depending on which stone you set down. Each trait line grants
its own rune-stone (more on those below).

## The Attunement Panel

The Rune-keeper's signature mechanic is **attunement.** Every skill you use
shifts your attunement along a scale:

- **Healing skills** attune toward the **healing side (green / right).**
- **Damage skills** attune toward the **damage side (red / left).**
- Some skills always attune toward **neutral.**

The more attuned you are toward one side, the more effective your skills of that
type become. There are also **cash-out skills** that consume all of your
attunement of a given type for a large payout, resetting you back to neutral.
Managing your attunement is central to playing the Rune-keeper well.

## Trait Trees

The Rune-keeper has three trait lines, and all three can be selected as your
primary trait tree. Each line also grants access to its own rune-stone. When you
first open the class trait panel, before you've committed to a line, it looks
like this:

![The Rune-keeper class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/rune-keeper/rune-keeper-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### Benediction of Peace — Healing (Blue)

The Blue line, **Benediction of Peace**, focuses on healing — especially
single-target healing and heals over time (HoTs). It grants access to the
**Rune of Restoration**, which restores morale to all allies within range.

![The Rune-keeper's Benediction of Peace (blue) trait tree, focused on healing](/images/LotRO/classes/rune-keeper/rune-keeper-class-traits-blue.png)

### Cleansing Flame — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **Cleansing Flame**, focuses on damage — especially burning damage
and longer-range, slower-cast skills. It grants access to the **Volcanic
Rune-stone**, which deals damage over time to enemies within range. This is your
primary group damage line.

![The Rune-keeper's Cleansing Flame (red) trait tree, focused on burning damage](/images/LotRO/classes/rune-keeper/rune-keeper-class-traits-red.png)

### Solitary Thunder — Burst Damage (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **Solitary Thunder**, focuses on shorter-range, instant-cast
damage skills and stuns. It grants access to the **Fulgurite Rune-stone**,
which, after a few seconds, damages and stuns enemies within range.

![The Rune-keeper's Solitary Thunder (yellow) trait tree, focused on instant-cast damage and stuns](/images/LotRO/classes/rune-keeper/rune-keeper-class-traits-yellow.png)

## A Note on Rune-stones

Because each trait line grants a different rune-stone, experienced Rune-keepers
often spend trait points in the **other** lines specifically to gain access to
those rune-stones — mixing and matching their placeable tools beyond whatever
line they've chosen as their primary.
