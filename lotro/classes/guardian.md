The Guardian is the quintessential tank — the first tank class introduced to
the game, and still a rock-solid pick. It's a pure melee class that strikes a
great balance between good damage and strong survivability.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Free class |
| **Armour** | Heavy |
| **Primary stat** | Might |
| **Roles** | Tank · Damage (plus a support line) |
| **Playstyle** | Pure melee — close combat |

## Is the Guardian Right for You?

Its damage line isn't competitive with the dedicated damage classes, but the
balance it strikes between dealing damage and staying alive makes it a **great
overall first class** for anyone who enjoys close combat and is interested in
tanking. It's fairly easy to learn, and because it's so sturdy, it's forgiving
while you find your feet.

## Trait Trees

The Guardian has three trait lines: **two can be selected as your primary trait
tree**, and the third is a support line that strengthens the other two. When
you first open the class trait panel, before you've committed to a line, it
looks like this:

![The Guardian class trait panel before any trait line has been selected](/images/LotRO/classes/guardian/guardian-class-traits-selection-panel.png)

### The Defender of the Free — Tank (Blue)

The Blue line, **The Defender of the Free**, is focused on tanking. If you play
a Guardian and want to group with others, **this is the line you'll want to
learn to play.**

![The Guardian's Defender of the Free (blue) trait tree, focused on tanking](/images/LotRO/classes/guardian/guardian-class-traits-blue.png)

### The Keen Blade — Damage (Red)

The Red line, **The Keen Blade**, focuses on damage — particularly bleed
damage. **This is the trait line I recommend for levelling.**

![The Guardian's Keen Blade (red) trait tree, focused on bleed damage](/images/LotRO/classes/guardian/guardian-class-traits-red.png)

### The Fighter of Shadow — Support (Yellow)

The Yellow line, **The Fighter of Shadow**, supports the other two trait lines.
At higher levels you'll find many good traits here to increase both your damage
and your defences.

![The Guardian's Fighter of Shadow (yellow) trait tree, which supports the other two lines](/images/LotRO/classes/guardian/guardian-class-traits-yellow.png)
