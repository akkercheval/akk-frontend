# LotRO Points  
LotRO Points are The Lord of the Rings Online's premium currency.  There are four ways to acquire LotRO Points:  
- Earn in game by completing Deeds.  
- Purchase directly through the in-game LotRO Store.  
- VIP accounts gain 500 LotRO Points each month.  
- Supporter Packs and Expansion Packs contain LotRO Points in addition to other items and content.
