# Survival Instincts — A Player&rsquo;s Guide

> An unofficial, player-made companion to the **Survival Instincts** system
> introduced with _The Wolves of Mordor_. This page starts from the in-game
> welcome letter every character received at launch on **Wednesday, October 28,
> 2026**, then walks through the new Trait Tree Panel and the skills each group
> shares. It will keep growing as I explore the new content.

## Getting Started

Speak to Orthonn near the stables (22.4N, 53.7W) and begin *1.1: A Storm in Late Summer*.  The quest chain will teleport you to the new region of Mordor.  Work through the epic story and other quests as you encounter them.  You will gain trait points to spend as you complete quests in the area.

## Survival Instincts

Throughout Draugardh you will meet members of the Khemoz&ecirc;l, Scouts of
Draugardh, and T&ecirc;tikir people. Each group will share skills and lessons they
have learned while living in Draugardh and they will share access to these skills
and lessons with you. You will also find ways to augment your personal skills and
those earned from these groups through your travels.

### Accessing Survival Instincts

- Open your traits panel.
- Select the tab for **Survival Instincts**.
- Note that **Wolves of Mordor** is selected.

### Assigning Survival Points

- Click on the traits you want to improve. This will add points from the pool
  listed on the page to the traits you have clicked. You can also **Shift +
  Left-click** to assign **10** pool points to a Survival Instinct trait.
- If you would like to remove points, you can **right-click**. To remove ten
  points at a time, use **Shift + Right-click**.
- Confirm that you want to assign points to these traits by hitting the **Apply**
  button.

### Activating Survival Instincts

- Drag a Survival Instinct trait from the passive field and assign it to an active
  slot.
- The Survival Instinct is now active and ready for use.
