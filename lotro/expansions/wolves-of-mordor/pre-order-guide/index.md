# The Wolves of Mordor — Pre-Order Guide

> An unofficial, player-made look at what comes in each pre-order package for
> _The Wolves of Mordor_ — and, more importantly, how the cosmetics actually
> look across the different races before you spend your money.

Standing Stone Games always shows their cosmetics on a race or two, but rarely
on every build, and almost never on hobbits — which is what I play most. Because
humans, elves, dwarves, hobbits, Beornings, high-elves, and river-hobbits all
have very different proportions, a set that looks sharp on one race can look odd
on another. This guide collects my own in-game screenshots so you can judge for
yourself.

A few notes before the galleries:

- The **Were-wolf Hunter&rsquo;s** gear dyes well. The **Dauntless Commander&rsquo;s**
  gear does **not** dye well, so I&rsquo;ve left most of it undyed.
- The **horse/pony mounts** are **not dyeable**. The **war-steed cosmetics**
  are mixed: the head-piece, leg-guards, and caparison dye (Navy shown), while
  the saddle and accessory do not. The tail uses the war-steed&rsquo;s hide
  colours and only tints the horse&rsquo;s tail hair, not the cosmetic itself.
- Several war-steed shots show a single piece equipped at a time, so you can see
  exactly what each slot adds.
- The female Beorning shots currently also include the Hunter&rsquo;s Hound pet.
  I&rsquo;ll swap in a cleaner isolated shot of the pet later.
