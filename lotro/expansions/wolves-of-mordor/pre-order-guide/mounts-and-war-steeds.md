# Mounts & War-steed Cosmetics

Included with the **Collector&rsquo;s** and **Ultimate** editions. The two
cosmetic **mounts** (the horse and pony) are **not dyeable**. The **war-steed
cosmetics** are a mix:

- **Dyeable:** head-piece, leg-guards, and caparison (shown here in Navy).
- **Not dyeable:** saddle and accessory.
- **Tail:** uses the war-steed&rsquo;s hide colour options rather than armour
  dyes. Applying a colour (Black in these screenshots) tints the horse&rsquo;s
  tail hair only — it does not change the colour of the tail cosmetic itself.

The per-piece war-steed previews are split onto their own pages (one per set) so
the heavier screenshots don&rsquo;t all load at once — use the links at the bottom.
