# Neighbours — A Player&rsquo;s Guide

> An unofficial, player-made companion to the **Neighbours** system introduced
> with _The Wolves of Mordor_. This page focuses on the mechanics I&rsquo;ve
> worked out in-game — how to unlock neighbours, earn reputation, and where to
> find each one — **without spoiling any quest stories or character secrets**.

Standing Stone Games&rsquo; own
[Neighbours overview](https://www.lotro.com/guides/lotro-neighbours-guide-en)
is the best place to read the design intent behind the feature. This guide fills
in the practical details a new neighbour-collector actually needs: how selection
boxes work, how reputation adds up, and the landscape and Gift Maker locations
for each of the four launch neighbours.

> **⚠️ Low-level characters, beware the landscape locations.** Some neighbours
> sit in **high-level zones**. **Brútha**, for example, is out in a **level 115**
> area near **Lake-town**. A low-level character who ports there (for instance by
> completing a quest) will have **no safe way to travel out on foot** &mdash; your
> only real exit is to **map away** to a safer destination. Straying far from
> Lake-town is dangerous.
>
> That word is literal here: much of this region is flagged as a **Dangerous
> Place**. In a Dangerous Place, enemies notice and **attack from a much greater
> distance than normal**, and if you are defeated you don&rsquo;t just revive
> nearby &mdash; you **retreat all the way back to your milestone location**. Keep
> a map route handy before you visit.

## Unlocking Neighbours

Right now the only way to gain access to neighbours is through one of the
**Wolves of Mordor** expansion packages:

| Edition | Selection boxes | Neighbours total |
|---|---|:---:|
| Standard | One **double-selection** box (choose two) | 2 |
| Collector&rsquo;s | The double-selection box **plus one single-selection** box | 3 |
| Ultimate | The double-selection box **plus two single-selection** boxes | 4 |

The editions are cumulative. The **Standard** edition includes a single
double-selection box that lets you choose two different neighbours. The
**Collector&rsquo;s** edition adds one single-neighbour selection box on top of
that, and the **Ultimate** edition adds a second single-neighbour box — so an
Ultimate purchase gives you three boxes in all: the double-selection box plus two
single-selection boxes.

**Open the double-selection box first.** Unfortunately, every box lets you select
*any* neighbour, even one you already own, and there is no safeguard against
picking a duplicate. Choosing carefully matters for that reason. Opening the
double-selection box first shows you everyone still available and keeps you from
accidentally re-picking a neighbour with one of the single-selection boxes.

Once you unlock a neighbour on your account, **any character on any server** can
visit that neighbour&rsquo;s **Gift Maker** and complete a short quest to receive
another housing summoning item — handy for summoning the same neighbour at a
house on a different server.

## Earning Neighbour Reputation

Your standing with each neighbour is tracked on the **Neighbour Info** tab of the
Houses panel.

Reputation is tracked **per server**. Every character on the same server
contributes to that server&rsquo;s shared reputation with a neighbour, but each
server keeps its own separate total — so progress you make on one server does not
carry over to your characters on another.

There are a few ways to earn reputation:

| Activity | Reputation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Completing a neighbour quest (chapter) | **25** | Per quest, **per character** |
| Chatting with a summoned neighbour at your house | **10** | Once **per day** |
| Giving gifts | Varies | Depends on gift quality and the neighbour&rsquo;s likes/dislikes |

You can also receive a **gift box from each neighbour once per account, per
week**, so it&rsquo;s worth checking in on everyone regularly.

Because quest completions pay out **per character**, players with several alts
on the same server can earn that server&rsquo;s reputation much faster by running
each neighbour&rsquo;s available chapters on multiple characters.

Generally speaking, completing a neighbour quest ports you to that
neighbour&rsquo;s landscape location. The one exception I&rsquo;ve run into is
**Haldo**, whose quest takes you to **Gondamon in Ered Luin** instead.

## Friendship Status & Chapter Unlocks

Reputation moves you through a series of friendship statuses, and new story
chapters unlock as you climb. Here&rsquo;s what I&rsquo;ve confirmed so far:

| Friendship status | Chapters unlocked |
|---|---|
| Stranger | Chapter 1, Chapter 2 |
| Acquaintance | Chapter 3 |
| Casual Friend | Chapter 4 |
| Companion | Chapter 5 |
| Friend | Chapter 6 |
| Close Friend | _(no new chapter)_ |
| Boon Companion | _(not yet confirmed)_ |
| Confidant | _(not yet confirmed)_ |

**Boon Companion** is the status that comes after Close Friend. Reaching
**Close Friend does _not_ unlock a new chapter** — the chapter unlocks pause
there. I haven&rsquo;t yet confirmed whether a chapter unlocks at Boon
Companion.

**Confidant** is the status that comes after Boon Companion. I haven&rsquo;t yet
confirmed whether a chapter unlocks at Confidant either.

### Reputation Needed to Advance

Friendship reputation is a **single cumulative total** that keeps climbing &mdash;
it does **not** reset between statuses. Each status unlocks when your lifetime
total reaches a set threshold. Here&rsquo;s what I&rsquo;ve confirmed so far:

| Status reached | Cumulative reputation |
|---|---|
| Stranger | **0** |  
| Acquaintance | **125** |  
| Casual Friend | ? |  
| Companion | ? |  
| Friend | **675** |
| Close Friend | **1,000** |
| Boon Companion | **1,500** |
| Confidant | **2,275** |

> **The reputation bar is cumulative &mdash; it does _not_ reset at each tier.**
> The progress bar on the Neighbour Info panel shows your **running lifetime
> total** on the left and the **next status&rsquo;s threshold minus one** on the
> right. So the bar fills continuously across your whole climb: the moment you
> reach Boon Companion at 1,500 the bar keeps right on counting toward Confidant,
> reading something like **1,513 / 2,274** (2,274 being one point below the
> 2,275 Confidant threshold).
>
> I originally thought the bar reset at every tier &mdash; it doesn&rsquo;t. When
> you tick over into a new status the number just keeps climbing toward the next
> threshold; it never drops back to zero.

Here&rsquo;s the cumulative bar in action. Brútha has just reached Boon Companion
and the bar already reads **1,513 / 2,274**, well on its way toward the 2,275
needed for Confidant &mdash; proof the total carries straight over rather than
resetting:

## Known Issues

- **Golwen on Legendary Servers — wrong exit port (no spoilers).** On the
  **Angmar Legendary Server**, **Golwen&rsquo;s Chapter 2** flagged my character
  (a level 103) with &ldquo;_You have entered a restricted area and will be
  removed if you do not leave_,&rdquo; yet I was still able to complete the
  instance normally. On exiting, though, I was returned to my starting town
  (**Mossward**) instead of being ported to **Dol Amroth** as expected. This
  looks like a Legendary Server&ndash;specific bug and may affect any of
  Golwen&rsquo;s quests. So far it hasn&rsquo;t affected any of the other
  neighbours.

The two quests below can also bug out, but each has a workaround. The fixes
involve small quest-step spoilers, so they&rsquo;re tucked inside the toggles
below — click to expand only if you want to read them.

<details>
<summary><strong>Golwen&rsquo;s Chapter 6 — quest bug &amp; workaround (spoilers)</strong></summary>

**Golwen&rsquo;s Chapter 6** can bug after you help her search for something and
then talk to her. To avoid the bug, follow her closely until she stops. Watch the
seagull fly off, then follow it up the stairs. The quest should update with:

> The seagull seems to have left something behind&hellip;

There should then be a glowing spot down the stairs, near the fountain, that you
can click on. When the quest is bugged, this glowing spot cannot be clicked.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Emmathron&rsquo;s Chapter 4 — quest bug &amp; workaround (spoilers)</strong></summary>

**Emmathron&rsquo;s Chapter 4** can also bug. He will ask you to take a look at
his sketchbook, then walk away toward a tree. **Do not click the sketchbook until
he stops walking** — clicking too early will bug the quest.

</details>
