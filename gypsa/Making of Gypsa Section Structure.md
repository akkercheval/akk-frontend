# Making of Gypsa Section Structure
Possibly make this section flow like a book:
Making of Gypsa
    Introduction
        ↓
    Character Bible
        ↓
    Production Guide
        ↓
    Design Explorations
        ↓
    Reference Library
        ↓
    Milestones

One thing I would definitely preserve is the feeling that visitors are walking through the forest.
Instead of thinking:
    "Next page"
I'd think:
    "Next stop on the trail."
Even little touches like:
    "Continue down the trail →"
    "Back to the crossroads ←"
    "Nearby discoveries"
    "Follow Gypsa's footprints"

would reinforce the Friendly Forest theme without becoming gimmicky.    

Imagine that every major page ends with a little handwritten-looking panel:
    🌿 Gypsa's Trail Notes
    What did we learn on this stretch of the trail?
    Example, your Muzzle Exploration page might end with:
        *Today we discovered that curiosity isn't just an expression; it's built into the way Gypsa's muzzle reaches toward the world. We also learned that warmth comes more from flowing transitions than from shortening the muzzle.*

## 2. Character
    This is almost entirely the Character Bible.

    Not the whole document.

    A readable summary.

    Things like:
    - Personality
    - Values
    - Philosophy
    - Design Intent
    - Friendly Forest
    - Equipment
    - Emergent Characteristics (eventually)

    This answers:
        *"Who is Gypsa?"*
## 3. Foundations
    This page would probably become ChatGPT's favorite section.

    It's where you explain the engineering.

    Include things like:
        - Production Guide
        - Canonical Body Dummy
        - Design Hierarchy
        - Design Invariants
        - Every Curve Has a Job
        - Every Relationship Has a Reason
        - Lessons Learned workflow

    This is where developers and artists alike will probably slow down and say, "Oh...this is how they're approaching it."
## 4. Explorations
    This is where all of our worksheets live.
    Each exploration becomes its own little story.
    For example:

    **Head Exploration**
        Inside:
        - Phase 1
        - Phase 2
        - Eyes
        - Ears
        - Expressions

    Each page would show:
    - the question
    - the sketches
    - the lessons learned
    - the resulting decision

    I love the idea of ending every exploration with a small "What We Learned" box rather than just "Winner"

    That reinforces your retrospective mindset.
## 5. Production
    This becomes practical.
    The actual approved references.
    For example:
        Approved References
        gypsa-base.svg
        canonical-body-dummy.svg
        palette.svg
        construction.svg
        turnaround.svg
    Almost like an SDK.

    I think developers will smile when they realize that's what you've done.

## 6. Gallery
    Finished work only.
    Expressions.
    Seasonal art.
    Holiday versions.
    Little comics.
    Animations.
    This is where visitors simply enjoy Gypsa.
    No engineering.
    No process.
    Just the finished character.
