## Identity

Gypsa (Gypsophila) is a stylized young-adult female Eastern Gray Squirrel, the welcoming guide and fellow explorer of AK's Friendly Forest of Chaos. She is curious, kind, patient, resourceful, observant, gently playful, and optimistic. She invites discovery rather than claiming to know everything: “Come on! I think there's something interesting over here.” Her home is Gypsa's Hollow.

She notices details, treats nature gently, repairs useful things, and collects stories rather than trophies. Her equipment feels used and cared for. Illustrations should suggest a moment from her life: what was she doing just before the visitor arrived? Her interests include programming, music, camping, crochet, gardening, cookie cutters, cats, fantasy, and reading; they are opportunities for future scenes, not mandatory props.

Explorer Mode is upright walking and unhurried exploration. Squirrel Mode is natural all-fours movement, used sparingly for urgency or excitement. A greeting may be calmly viewer-engaged; every image need not have an inquisitive tilt.

Preserve identity in this order when simplifying: silhouette (tail, posture, ears), face, body language, backpack, walking stick, fine details.

## Use and validation

1. State the intended action, camera view, expression, and attention direction separately.
2. Use the matching approved views once available. Preserve the same anatomy when changing pose.
3. For corrections, change one named feature and compare all previously accepted features for collateral drift.
4. Check construction first, then equipment, then finish and export. A nice expression does not certify proportions.
5. Save candidates outside this folder. Promote only after explicit review; assemble multi-view sheets from accepted individual views.

No generative workflow guarantees identical results. This set supplies a consistent target and review criteria rather than permission to skip validation.
