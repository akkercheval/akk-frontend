# Gypsa Character Bible
*Version 1.0 (Draft)*
## 1. Purpose

Gypsa is the official mascot of AK's Friendly Forest of Chaos.  
  
She serves as a welcoming guide, companion, and storyteller, inviting visitors to explore the website as if they are wandering through a friendly woodland full of interesting discoveries.  

She's a fellow explorer who's just a step ahead on the trail and an enthusiastic learner.  

She's not saying, 
    *"Follow me because I know everything."*

She's saying,
    *"Come on! I think there's something interesting over here."*
  
Gypsa is not simply an illustration; she is a recurring character whose personality and visual identity remain consistent across every page.  

## 2. Core Identity

**Name:** Gypsa  
**Species:** Stylized Eastern Gray Squirrel  
**Role:** Explorer, collector of stories, woodland guide  
**Home:** The Friendly Forest of Chaos  
**Occupation:** Discovering, making, learning, and sharing.  

## 3. Character Philosophy

**Gypsa believes:**
- Every trail has something worth noticing.  
- Every object has a story.  
- Curiosity is more valuable than speed.  
- Craftsmanship matters.  
- Repairs are signs of care.  
- Kindness is strength.  
- Nature deserves respect.  

She is an explorer more than an adventurer.  

She seeks understanding more than simple excitement.  

### 4. Personality

- Curious
- Patient
- Gentle
- Resourceful
- Thoughtful
- Observant
- Creative
- Quietly playful
- Welcoming
- Optimistic

She enjoys helping others discover things for themselves rather than showing off what she knows.

## 5. Visual Identity
**Overall Style**
- Friendly

**Design Goals**

## 6. Body Language

### Default posture:
- upright
- slight forward lean
- relaxed smile
- calm stance
- expressive tail
- open posture

### Signature Expression  
**Inquisitive:** asymmetric brow, attentive eyes, slight head tilt, and one ear forward  

### Movement modes:

**Explorer Mode**
- Walking upright with walking stick.
- Enjoying the journey.
- Stopping often.

**Squirrel Mode**
- Runs naturally on all fours.
- Used sparingly.
- Reserved for moments requiring urgency or excitement.

## 7. Tail Language

The tail is one of Gypsa's primary expressive features.
It communicates emotion, movement, and interaction.

**Examples:**
- Relaxed
- Curious
- Excited
- Thinking
- Wrapped around sheet music
- Supporting a laptop
- Helping balance while hiking
- Curled around her feet while resting

The tail should interact naturally with the environment whenever possible.

## 8. Equipment
### Backpack *(canonical — see `Gypsa Backpack.md`)*
- Woodland backpack.  
- Acorn-shaped leather flap (quilted cap texture, no stem).  
- Drawstring closure.  
- Two leather shoulder straps, sewn to the back.  
- Leather top loop for hanging.  
- Enlarged oak leaf patch on the front.  
- Visible, careful repairs.  
- Signs of careful use.  
- Acorn-shaped cookie cutter tied to the top loop as a charm.  
- Side-mounted found-oak walking stick on two leather straps.  
- Acorn cap-inspired crocheted hat for cold winter months. 

The backpack changes slowly over the years through useful additions and keepsakes.  

### Bandana *(canonical — see `Gypsa Bandana.md`)*
- Square cloth bandana.  
- Folded corner to corner into a right triangle.  
- Tied around the neck with a small square knot in the front.  
- Solid oak-leaf green (`#4F7A3A`), matching the backpack's oak leaf patch.  
- A simple, everyday, well-loved accent.  

### Walking Stick *(canonical structure — see `Gypsa Walking Stick.md`)*
- Found, not bought.  
- Naturally fallen oak branch.  
- Cloth grip.  
- Useful string.  
- Occasional button.  
- Seasonal leaf.  
- Natural fork near the top where she sometimes keeps a small keepsake.  
- A trusted companion.  

## 9. Objects Have Stories

Every recurring object should have a reason for existing.

Nothing is decorative without purpose.

**Examples:**
- The cookie cutter, a gift from her grandmother reminds Gypsa of baking.
- The notebook records discoveries.
- The clarinet represents making music in the forest.
- The walking stick has slowly evolved over years of wandering.

## 11. Illustration Principles

- Illustrations should feel like moments from Gypsa's life.
- Not advertisements.
- Not poses.
- She should appear naturally occupied when visitors arrive.
- Almost every illustration should answer the question:
    "What was Gypsa doing just before we arrived?"

## 12. Things Gypsa Always Does

- Notices little details.
- Repairs useful things.
- Collects stories.
- Shares discoveries.
- Welcomes visitors.
- Treats nature gently.
- Enjoys making things.
- Continues learning.

## 13. Things Gypsa Never Does

- Acts cruelly.
- Shows off.
- Destroys nature without reason.
- Collects trophies.
- Rushes without purpose.
- Throws useful things away.

## 14. Design Language

- Rounded geometry.
- Consistent outlines.
- Minimal detail.
- Flat colors.
- Expressive eyes.
- Tail-driven animation.
- Natural asymmetry.
- SVG optimized.

## 15. Evolution

- Gypsa grows with the website.
- The website grows with Gypsa.
- Neither remains static.
- Future additions should feel earned through new stories rather than arbitrary redesigns.

## 16. Motto
*"There's always something interesting just around the next tree."*
also
*"Explore broadly. Decide deliberately."*

It captures Gypsa's optimism, curiosity, and love of exploration. It's also a lovely invitation to visitors: there is always another page to discover, another hobby to learn about, or another little detail tucked away in the Friendly Forest of Chaos.

### 19. Design Hierarchy

*Not every feature is equally important. If we ever simplify Gypsa for a favicon, embroidery, laser engraving, or vinyl decal, we should know what to preserve first.*

1. Silhouette — oversized tail, upright posture, ears.
2. Face — kind eyes, gentle smile, expressive brows.
3. Body Language — slight forward lean, open posture.
4. Backpack — acorn flap, oak leaf patch.
5. Walking Stick — trusted companion.
6. Fine Details — cookie cutter charm, stitching, patches, string, buttons.

That hierarchy gives us a clear strategy for creating simplified versions without losing Gypsa's identity.
