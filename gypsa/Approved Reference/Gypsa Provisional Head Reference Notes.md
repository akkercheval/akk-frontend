## 2. Where it deviates from perfect (resolve before final promotion)

| # | Deviation | Canon reference | Severity | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | **Below-eye gray clearance thin on the near eye.** The warm muzzle marking rises close to the near lower lid; the gray strip is present but not clearly continuous. | Head & Expression → "Below-eye clearance — canonical"; Rendering Standard → facial color pattern | **Minor canon miss** — the one true remaining canon item | Lower the warm boundary slightly beneath the near eye for a continuous visible gray strip. Boundary move only; do not move/resize eyes. |
| P2 | **Muzzle slightly short/stubby.** Reads as a squirrel but is a touch shorter than ideal on-model. | Head & Expression → "Muzzle volume" | **Optional polish** | Could extend forward a hair. Acceptable as-is; flagged so it is not treated as final-locked. |
| P3 | **Peripheral fur long/jagged** along neck and lower jaw. | Rendering Standard → "Foreground style" (gentle contour breaks, not sawteeth) | **Cosmetic** | Soften/shorten ~30–40% if desired. Crown and ears excluded. |
| P4 | **Faint near-side muzzle contour loop** remains near the mouth (reduced from v3). | Rendering Standard → clean contour | **Very minor** | Simplify to a single clean contour if touched again. |

### Open canon items this reference does NOT resolve (not defects — undecided in the docs)
- **Outline weight** — now canon: 2.35% of the calibration span per Rendering Standard → "Outline weight — canon". This image's measured outline has not been checked against that ratio; measure it before final promotion. Possible slight reduction of interior detail lines is still under consideration by the user and is not canon.
- **Exact below-eye clearance width**, **facial-marking boundary pixels**, and **shadow tones** — flagged pending in the Rendering Standard. P1 corrects a visible deficiency without inventing final pixel values.

- **Use now:** as the three-quarter visual anchor for the R1 head turnaround (front + profile generated to match this head), and as a shape/proportion reference for subsequent head work.
- **Do not treat as final** until P1 (and optionally P2–P4) are resolved and the result is re-checked against the Final full-image acceptance check in `../Gypsa Head Refinement 20261031 Correction Pass.md`.
