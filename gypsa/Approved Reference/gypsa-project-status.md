# Gypsa's Project Status

## Character: Gypsa

### Character Summary  
- Young adult squirrel  
- Curious     
- Kind  
- Patient  
- Explorer  
- Walking stick  
- Backpack  
- Green neck bandana  
- Loves discovering things  
- Invites others to explore  

### Design Intent:  
*Create a stylized eastern gray squirrel mascot who is recognizable, approachable, expressive, and easy to consistently render while preserving a quiet sense of curiosity.*  

### Design Principles  
- Every curve has a job.  
- Every relationship has a reason.  
- Foundation before finish.  
- Design relationships before features.  
- Discover before decorating.   
- Validate before approving.
