## Decision Summary

### Accepted

- Organic silhouettes feel more authentic than perfectly geometric ones.
- Youthfulness is conveyed primarily through forehead and cheek proportions.
- Mature chin proportions move away from Gypsa's intended personality.

### Deferred

- Ear placement
- Muzzle construction
- Fur silhouette

### Rejected

- Canonical teardrop silhouette
- Elongated mature chin

### New Principles

- Evaluate every design both in isolation and in context.
- Prefer organic authenticity over geometric perfection.
