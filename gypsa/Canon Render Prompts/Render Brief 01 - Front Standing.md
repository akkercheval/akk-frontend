# Render Brief 01 — Gypsa, Front Standing (Walking Stick + Backpack)

**Use with:** the shared **Gypsa Canonical Design Brief** (Section A of
`Gypsa Rendering Plan.md`). Paste that first, then this brief, then attach the
reference images listed below.

---

## The one goal of this render

> A single, clean, full-body "hero" portrait of Gypsa **standing upright and
> facing forward (straight-on)**, **holding her walking stick in one hand** with
> its tip resting on the ground beside her, and **wearing her backpack** (straps
> visible over both shoulders, the pack itself behind her). Calm, welcoming
> default expression. This is the definitive front reference for the character.

Everything else in this brief serves that one goal. Do not add scenery, extra
props, text, or alternate poses.

---

## Pose specification

- **Facing:** straight-on front view, looking at the viewer (gaze just past the
  camera is fine — that's her default).
- **Stance:** standing upright on both hind feet, weight even, feet planted flat
  (plantigrade), a **slight friendly forward lean.** Relaxed, open, welcoming —
  not stiff or at attention.
- **Head:** small ~7° tilt, default "I found something interesting" expression:
  eyes slightly widened with interest, one brow a touch higher, small
  closed-mouth smile.
- **Tail:** big bushy S-sweep rising up behind/beside her, clearly visible around
  her silhouette (a squirrel's tail reads even from the front — let it peek out
  to one side and above).

## Walking stick (authority = `Approved Reference/Gypsa Canonical Walking Stick Reference.png`)

- Structure is canon: a found oak branch with a natural Y-fork near the top,
  ground-to-eyeline height, at the sturdy spec thickness shown in the reference
  (as of 2026-10-02 the reference is correctly thickened — match it, don't thin
  it). Decorative details (grip wrap, keepsakes) are still undecided; treat those
  as a proposal, not canon.
- Held in **one hand** (her right hand / viewer's left is fine), fingers **curled
  around it in a real grip** — remember she has three fingers + an opposed thumb,
  so the grip must look like a hand closing on the stick, not a paw.
- The stick is a **found, naturally fallen oak branch:** organic, slightly
  irregular thickness, a little character in its bends. **Not** a smooth machined
  dowel and **not** a tall wizard staff.
- Height: from the **ground up to roughly her eye level.** Tip rests on the
  ground beside her foot; she is holding it, not leaning heavily on it.
- Detailing (keep subtle): a **cloth grip** wrapped where she holds it, a bit of
  string, maybe one small button or a single seasonal leaf, and a **natural fork
  near the top.** Understated and well-loved.

## Backpack (authority = `Approved Reference/Canonical Backpack Reference.png`)

- Worn on her back with **both shoulder straps visible** crossing over the front
  of her shoulders/chest; the pack body sits behind her (in a pure front view,
  the top of the pack and/or straps read; let a little of the pack show past her
  sides so the viewer knows it's there).
- Features: **acorn-cap flap**, **embroidered oak-leaf patch**, **drawstring
  closure**, and the small **silver acorn cookie-cutter charm** hanging from it.
- Well-loved and lightly mended, made of natural materials.

## Correct-the-drift checklist (repeat to the renderer)

- ✅ Short and **stocky**, rounded pear body — NOT tall/lanky.
- ✅ Ears **low-set, rounded, moderate** — NOT tall or pointed.
- ✅ Hands with **3 fingers + 1 opposed thumb**, gripping the stick like a hand —
  NOT mittens, NOT four fingers, NOT a paw.
- ✅ **No visible neck** — head flows straight into the back/chest.
- ✅ Big **bushy S-sweep tail**, fur leaning toward the tip — NOT thin, NOT a
  smooth tube, NOT scraggly.
- ✅ **Flat colors, even outline, transparent/plain background** — NOT glossy, 3D,
  or gradient-shaded.
- ✅ Exact palette from the shared brief.

## Proportion note (keep her stocky)

The canonical structural work makes Gypsa deliberately **shorter and stockier**
than a typical squirrel (canonical back length 1.70H, hip fixed low; see the
proportions column on the v2.0 sheet, which uses head-units up to 4.60H at the
ear tips). If the renderer must choose, favor the **shorter, rounder, stocky**
silhouette. Do not stretch her tall and lanky.
