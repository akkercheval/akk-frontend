# Gypsa Full-Body Validation — All-Fours Pose Prompt

**Purpose:** Third full-body validation of Gypsa with all canonical equipment, in
her **all-fours "Squirrel Mode"** stance — the run-on-all-fours pose the Character
Bible reserves for moments of urgency or excitement. This validates that the same
frozen skeleton re-articulates into a quadruped stance, that the **walking stick
rides stowed on the pack** (she cannot hold it on all fours), and that the **tail
still roots at the spine base** in a horizontal-back pose.

**Chosen pose:** Gypsa **on all fours in a side / profile 3/4 view**, in a calm
walking or about-to-dash stance (not a frantic sprint) — all four limbs on or near
the ground, back roughly horizontal, head lowered and forward, looking ahead or
slightly up toward the viewer with her curious expression. Walking stick **stowed
on the side of the backpack**; tail sweeping up and back.

**Status:** Exploration / validation. Builds on the accepted provisional upright
and back poses (`../../Approved Reference/Gypsa Provisional Full Body Upright
Pose.png`, `../../Approved Reference/Gypsa Provisional Full Body Back Pose.png`)
and the specs `Gypsa Backpack.md`, `Gypsa Bandana.md`, `Gypsa Walking Stick.md`.
It also follows the all-fours structural rig
`../../Approved Reference/gypsa_all-fours_reference.svg`. If this prompt and those
references disagree, the rig and specs govern.

**Scale unit:** 1H = head height (chin to cranial top, excluding ears).

---

## 1. What to render

**One character — Gypsa — full body, on all fours, in a side / profile 3/4 view.**
Calm and curious (a gentle walk or a poised "about to scamper off," **not** a
manic sprint). Plain white / transparent background. No scene, no text, one
character only.

The camera should read her whole quadruped silhouette clearly — horizontal back,
all four limbs, the pack on her back with the stowed stick, and the big tail
arcing up behind her. Keep her face readable (head lowered but turned enough to
show her expression).

---

## 2. All-fours pose geometry (follow the structural rig)

The pose is the **same skeleton as upright, re-articulated** — do **not** change
any segment lengths, only the joint angles. Per
`gypsa_all-fours_reference.svg`:

- **Back is horizontal.** The spine points Nc → Sc → Pc → Tr all sit at about the
  **same height (~1.05H above the ground)** — a level back line, not an upright
  torso.
- **All four limbs reach the ground.** Both **front paws** (wrist→fingers) and
  both **hind feet** (ball→toes) plant on or near the ground line. The squirrel
  Z-fold hind leg (knee forward, hock back) and the folded foreleg carry the same
  canonical lengths as upright.
- **Head is lowered and forward**, dropping down/forward off the front of the
  spine; the muzzle points forward and slightly down. She can tip her head up a
  little to meet the viewer's eye.
- **Ears** point up and back off the lowered head.
- This is a **compact, gentle quadruped** — keep her proportions consistent with
  the two accepted upright poses (do not stretch her long and low like a realistic
  squirrel, and do not chibi her).

---

## 3. Carry over everything already validated

Match the **character** to the accepted provisional poses exactly; only the stance
and camera change.

- **Proportions:** compact young adult, same head-to-body relationship as the
  upright/back poses. Not chibi, not lanky.
- **Soft feminine build:** rounded, soft silhouette (pear-shaped torso when seen,
  fuller cheeks, short rounded muzzle).
- **Gray-dominant coat** `#595652`; warm tan `#B97A3D` only on the face mask,
  chest/belly, and inner ears (`#E7B37A`). Do not over-brown the coat.
- **Colors:** nose `#2B2B2B`, eyes `#111111`, claws `#FFFFFF`, outline `#1E1E1E`.
- Four fingers per front paw, three toes per hind foot; flat Friendly-Forest
  style.

---

## 4. Ears — Soft Taper, on the SIDES, NOT turned forward (watch item)

Keep the corrected ears from the back pose (authority `Gypsa Canonical Ear.png`):

- moderate size, broad base, **tapering to a soft point**, gentle inward curve —
  not rounded blobs, not sharp spikes.
- mounted **low on the sides of the skull**; on the lowered all-fours head they
  angle **up and back**, following the head — they must **NOT lean/turn forward**
  over the muzzle.

---

## 5. Tail — MUST attach at the tail root (critical, and different in this pose)

- The tail must emerge from the **terminal spine at the BASE of her back — the
  tail root (Tr), on the (now horizontal) spine, integrated into the rump** — and
  sweep **up and back**, arcing high above the level back in her canonical
  S-curve (see the rig's tail centerline).
- It must **NEVER** attach to the side of the hip, thigh, haunch, or rump surface.
- In this quadruped pose the rump is at the rear of a horizontal body; the tail
  still grows from the spine's end there, not from the top of a hind leg.

---

## 6. Backpack (canonical — `Gypsa Backpack.md`)

Worn on her back — on all fours the pack sits on top of her horizontal back,
riding between the shoulders and the tail root.

- **Canvas body** (`#A8743A`); **acorn-shaped leather flap** (`#6B4A2E`) with a
  rounded **cap** of **quilted diamond-lattice stitching** and a **nut** tapering
  to a soft point, **no stem**; **drawstring** under the flap.
- **Two leather shoulder straps** sewn to the **back** of the pack, wrapping around
  her shoulders/chest (on all fours these run forward around the front legs'
  shoulders).
- **Leather top loop** with the **small metal ACORN-shaped cookie cutter** (hollow,
  silver `#B8B8B8`) tied to it.
- Single **oak-leaf patch** (`#4F7A3A`) on the canvas.
- Keep it **trim in depth** so it sits neatly on her back and the tail clears it.

---

## 7. Walking stick — STOWED on the side (canonical — `Gypsa Walking Stick.md`)

She is on all fours, so the stick is **carried, not held**:

- The **full-length found-oak branch** runs **along one side** of the backpack,
  held by **two plain leather straps** (no holster, boot, or toggle).
- Natural branch: thicker slightly-swollen base, gentle waviness, a couple of
  subtle knots, and an **asymmetric two-arm Y-fork** near one end — the **shorter
  fork arm ≈ 60% of the longer arm's length**.
- Shown at believable full length (it may extend past the pack outline). Not a
  stub. Because the body is horizontal, the stowed stick reads roughly horizontal
  along the pack, following her back.

---

## 8. Bandana (canonical — `Gypsa Bandana.md`)

Green square cloth folded into a triangle, tied at the neck with a **small square
knot in FRONT**; solid oak-leaf green `#4F7A3A`. On the lowered all-fours head the
knot and draping point hang down at the front of her throat/chest; the band wraps
the back of the neck under the pack straps. It must not cover her face.

---

## 9. Rendering style

Flat **Friendly Forest** style: clean linework, rounded joins, `#1E1E1E` outlines,
canonical line weight ≈ 2.35% of head height, flat colors, no gradients, no
photorealism, no texture. Plain / transparent background. Warm and handcrafted.

---

## 10. Must / must-not checklist

**Must:**
- one Gypsa, full body, **on all fours**, side / profile 3/4 view, plain
  background
- **horizontal back**, all four limbs on/near the ground, same segment lengths as
  upright (re-articulated), head lowered and forward
- proportions and soft feminine build consistent with the two upright poses
- **ears soft-taper, side-mounted, angled up/back, NOT turned forward**
- **tail attached at the tail root** (base of the horizontal spine), arcing up and
  back, clearing the pack
- backpack on her back: acorn quilted flap, top loop + **acorn** charm, single
  oak-leaf patch, back-mounted straps, trim depth
- **walking stick stowed along one side** on **two plain leather straps**, full
  length, asymmetric fork (shorter arm ≈60% of the longer)
- gray-dominant coat; green bandana with a front square knot `#4F7A3A`
- four fingers / three toes; flat Friendly-Forest style

**Must not:**
- upright/bipedal stance (this pass is all fours) or a floating limb not reaching
  the ground
- realistic long-and-low squirrel proportions, or chibi/lanky proportions;
  masculine build
- ears turned/leaning forward, or rounded blob ears
- tail attached to the hip/thigh/haunch/rump surface, or a tail that fails to root
  at the spine base
- stick held in a paw (she is on all fours), stub stick, or holster/boot mount
- old backpack styling; oak-leaf cookie cutter (use **acorn**); duplicated
  oak-leaf patch
- frantic/violent action, motion-blur, text, labels, extra characters, or a
  background scene
