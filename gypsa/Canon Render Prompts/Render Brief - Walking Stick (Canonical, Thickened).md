## Paste-in prompt (give this to the renderer verbatim)

> Recreate the attached walking stick as a clean prop illustration. **Keep the
> exact same shape, proportions, bends, knots, and fork** as the attached image —
> this is a faithful redraw, not a new design. Make only two changes:
>
> **1. Make the branch noticeably thicker and sturdier.** The attached version is
> too thin and spindly. It should read as a sturdy-but-refined found oak branch —
> thinner than a club, thicker than a wand — using these proportions (where "H" =
> the height of one squirrel head, used only as a relative scale):
> - **Lower shaft, near the base:** diameter ≈ **0.15–0.16H** (slightly swollen foot).
> - **Grip zone** (the single bend partway up): diameter ≈ **0.14H**.
> - **Upper shaft, above the grip:** diameter ≈ **0.12–0.13H**.
> - **Fork arm tips:** diameter ≈ **0.09–0.11H**.
> - Taper smoothly and continuously from the thicker base up to the slender fork
>   tips, with a small natural thickening right at the grip bend.
> - As a rule of thumb: the whole stick should look roughly **1.6–1.8× thicker**
>   than the attached reference, while keeping the identical centerline and outline.
>
> **2. Put it on a fully transparent background.** No background color, no white
> card, no checkerboard pattern, no scenery, no drop shadow, no ground line —
> just the stick itself on transparency, as a clean cut-out PNG with an alpha
> channel.
>
> **Preserve exactly (do not change):**
> - The overall silhouette and the gentle natural waviness of the shaft.
> - The **single grip bend** partway up (one comfortable kink, not a hook, not a
>   repeating S-curve).
> - The **asymmetric Y-fork** at the top: two arms of clearly different length,
>   the **shorter arm ≈ 60% the length of the longer arm.** Keep the same arm that
>   is short and the same arm that is long as in the reference.
> - The **knots:** keep the same number and placement — roughly four subtle knots
>   up one side, with the two most visible ones (one near mid-shaft, one right at
>   the grip bend) doing most of the character work.
> - The **wood color and flat style:** natural oak brown, flat color fill (no
>   gradients, no photo texture, no painterly shading, no gloss), clean even
>   linework with a dark outline (`#1E1E1E`).
>
> **It must NOT be:** a straight machined dowel, a polished cane, a club, a
> wizard's staff, a thin wand, or a carved ornamental prop. No decorations (no
> cloth grip wrap, no string, no buttons, no leaves, no hanging charms) — this is
> the plain undecorated branch only.
>
> Output: a single vertical walking stick, centered, full height in frame with a

## Why only these two changes (reference)

Per `Approved Reference/Gypsa Walking Stick.md` §5 and the measured geometry in
`Gypsa Walking Stick Measurements.md` §5–6, the current reference's diameters
(grip ≈0.087H, lower shaft ≈0.08–0.11H, fork arms ≈0.05–0.06H) came out **thinner
than the canonical spec targets** (grip 0.14H, lower shaft 0.15–0.16H, fork arms
0.09–0.11H). The shape itself is accepted canon; the thinness is a rendering
artifact. This brief corrects the thickness to spec and fixes the opaque
background, and changes nothing else.
