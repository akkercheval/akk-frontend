## 2. Character (frozen — do not redesign)

Gypsa is a stylized young-adult **Eastern Gray Squirrel**. Apply her canonical
appearance from the supplied references. **Do not reinterpret her anatomy or
proportions.** Preserve:

- **Head:** rounded cranium, full cheeks, low-mounted soft-taper ears, broad
  egg-shaped muzzle that inflates from the skull, large "Quiet Observer" almond
  eyes, organic fur brows, soft layered crown fur. Default expression: eyes
  slightly widened with interest, one brow a touch higher, small closed-mouth
  smile, head tilt ~7°, looking just past the viewer.
- **Body:** compact, deliberately shorter than a typical squirrel (hobbit-short).
  Pear-shaped torso, minimal neck, upright posture with a slight forward lean.
- **Colors:** Fur `#595652`, Belly/face `#B97A3D`, inner ear `#E7B37A`, nose
  `#2B2B2B`, eyes `#111111`, claws `#FFFFFF`, outline `#1E1E1E`.
- **Hands/feet:** four fingers per hand, three toes per foot.
- **Line:** clean, consistent linework; flat colors; no gradients; rounded joins.

## 2b. Proportions — young adult, between the two attempts (critical)

Gypsa is a **young adult** — **neither a chibi cub (Pass 1) nor a lanky teenager
(Pass 2).** Pass 1 was too short and big-headed; Pass 2 was too tall and lean.
**Aim between them, closer to the canonical numbers below** — head size as in Pass
2 (good), but a **shorter, rounder, more compact body** than Pass 2. Canonical
proportions (1H = head height, chin to cranial top, excluding ears):

**Canonical landmark heights above the ground (upright):**

| Landmark | Height |
| --- | --- |
| Ground (feet) | 0.00H |
| Hip | 1.45H |
| **Shoulder** | **3.15H** |
| Base of head | 3.45H |
| Ear tips | 4.60H |

**What this means for the drawing:**

- The **head is about 1H tall** and the **shoulder sits ~3.15H above the ground** —
  so the head is only **about one-third of the shoulder height**, and the whole
  standing figure is roughly **4.6H tall to the ear tips** (~**2.5–3 heads tall to
  the shoulder**). She should read as a **slim, upright young adult**, not a
  big-headed cub.
- The **torso is long** — the shoulder-to-hip back span is **1.70H** (a full
  1.7 head-heights). Do not compress it. This length is what makes her
  "hobbit-short but grown," not stubby.
- The **legs are long enough to carry that height** — hip at 1.45H means the
  hip-to-ground run is nearly a head and a half. Do not draw short stubby legs.
- **"Compact / hobbit-short"** means she is shorter and gentler than a *realistic*
  squirrel — it does **not** mean chibi. Keep the head modest and the body tall.

**Quick self-check before finalizing:** head height should fit into the
crown-to-toe height roughly **4 to 4.5 times** (with ears, to the ear tips
~4.6×). Pass 1 was ~3.5× (too stubby); Pass 2 was ~5.6× (too lanky). **Target
~4.5×.** If she looks stretched and straight-sided, shorten the torso and legs
toward this range.

## 2c. Softness & feminine read (Pass 2 fix)

Pass 2 read as an "adolescent male" because the body was long, straight-sided,
and flat-chested with a longer muzzle. Gypsa is a **gentle young-adult female**;
recover that with **softness and roundness**, not by changing her identity:

- **Pear-shaped torso** — narrower at the shoulders, softly fuller/rounded through
  the lower belly and hips; **not** a long straight tube.
- **Fuller, rounded cheeks** — soft face, not a lean angular one.
- **Shorter, rounder muzzle** — do not lengthen or narrow the snout.
- **Softly rounded limbs and overall silhouette** — curves over straight lines.
- Keep her **compact** — the roundness plus the shorter build reads warm and
  feminine (as in Pass 1), while the corrected head size keeps her grown-up.

This is a matter of soft *silhouette and roundness*, not added markings, makeup,
eyelashes-as-costume, or any gendered styling cues.

## 2d. Ears — Soft Taper, mounted on the SIDES (recurring drift — top priority)

Canon ears are **"Soft Taper"** (see `Gypsa Canonical Ear.png`, the authority).
Shape:

- moderate size, **broad base**, rounded upper contour, **tapering to a soft
  point** at the top with a **gentle** inward curve
- **not** rounded domes/blobs (the Pass 2 error), and not sharp spikes

**Orientation (the Pass 3 error and a recurring historical drift):**

- The ears are mounted **low on the SIDES of the skull** and rise **up and
  slightly outward** — they must **NOT rotate/lean/turn forward over the face.**
- The inner ear should face mostly **outward to the side and slightly forward**,
  not aimed toward the viewer/forward like cupped listening ears.
- Picture the ears standing up off the top-sides of the head like a relaxed
  squirrel's, not folded or tilted toward the muzzle.
- If the ears look like they are "leaning in" toward the face or the front, they
  are turned too far forward — rotate them back to vertical / slightly outward.

## 3. Tail — MUST attach at the tail root (critical)

Gypsa's oversized S-curve tail is her signature silhouette. **Its attachment is
the single most important thing to get right in this pass.**

- The tail must emerge from the **terminal spine at the BASE of her back — the
  tail root (Tr), on the spine centerline, about 1.55H above the ground —
  integrated into the rump.**
- The tail must **NEVER** attach to the **side of the hip, the thigh, the haunch,
  or the rump surface.** It grows out of the base of the spine and sweeps up
  behind her in her canonical neutral S-curve.
- If drawing the tail would require attaching it to the hip or thigh to "look
  right," the attachment is wrong — keep it at the spine base.
