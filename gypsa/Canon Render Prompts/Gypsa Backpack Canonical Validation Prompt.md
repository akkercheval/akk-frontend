# Gypsa Backpack — Canonical Validation Render Prompt

**Purpose:** Regenerate the **single canonical backpack** (acorn flap,
side-mounted walking stick) with every refinement from the canonical spec applied,
so we can **visually validate** the design before it is drawn on Gypsa's body.

**Status:** Validation pass for the canonical backpack. This prompt implements
`../../Approved Reference/Gypsa Backpack.md`. If this prompt and that spec ever
disagree, the spec governs.

**What changed vs. the exploration render** (`ChatGPT Image Sep 22, 2026,
06_01_48 PM.png`, 3rd pack):
1. **Acorn nut lengthened** — the nut descends longer before tapering to the
   point (fuller sides, then curve to a soft point), instead of a quick teardrop.
2. **Oak-leaf body patch enlarged** — clearer presence on the pack front.
3. **Leather top loop added** — a hang loop at the top of the pack (was missing).
4. **Cookie-cutter charm = ACORN shape**, tied to the **top loop** — not the
   oak-leaf cutter, and not tied to the walking stick.
5. **No free-hanging medallion, no wooden toggle** between straps.

**Reference to attach:** `../../Approved Reference/Gypsa Walking Stick.md` (so the
stick reads as the full-length found-oak branch). Optionally attach the 06_01_48
render as a "keep this look, apply these changes" anchor. Do **not** attach body /

## 1. What to render

A **single woodland backpack, entirely by itself** (no character, no body, no
squirrel), in **3/4 view** — tilted so the **acorn top flap**, the **front face**,
and the **walking-stick side** are all visible. Plain white / transparent
background. One backpack only — no grids, labels, measurements, captions, or extra
objects.

## 3. The backpack, part by part

**Body:** cotton / linen **canvas** (`#A8743A`), sturdy and structured (holds its
shape). A **drawstring closure** gathers the mouth beneath the flap.

**Top flap — acorn (leather, `#6B4A2E`):**
- Shaped like an **acorn**: a broad rounded **cap** on top (upper ~40% of the
  flap) and a **nut** below it (lower ~60%) that **descends a good length with
  full sides before curving to a soft point** at the bottom. Not a quick teardrop.
- The **cap** carries **quilted cross-hatch (diamond-lattice) stitching** to
  replicate an acorn cap's texture. Stitching in `#1E1E1E`.
- **No stem** on the flap.
- Flap length = **half the pack height**.

**Shoulder straps:** **two leather straps** (`#6B4A2E`), stitched at the top and
running down the back.

**Leather top loop (hang loop):** a short **leather loop** at the top of the pack
(same dark brown as the flap, `#6B4A2E`), a little **thinner than the
walking-stick straps** (about 75% of their width). This is a normal backpack
hang-up loop.

**Cookie-cutter charm — acorn:** a **small metal cookie cutter in an ACORN
shape** — a **hollow metal cutter** (open outline, silver `#B8B8B8`), **not** a
solid medallion. It is **tied with a short knotted cord to the leather top loop**
(not to the walking stick).

**Mending — oak-leaf patch:** one **oak-leaf patch** (`#4F7A3A`) neatly sewn onto
the **canvas body**, **fully visible below the flap**, and **enlarged** so it has
clear presence (roughly a third of the visible body-front height). Tidy visible
stitching in `#1E1E1E`. Optionally one small additional neat repair (a restitched
seam) to show careful use.

**Walking stick — side-mount:** a **full-length found-oak walking stick** carried
**vertically down one side** of the pack, held by **two plain leather straps**
(no holster, no boot, no toggle). The branch is a natural found-oak stick: thicker
tapering base, gentle waviness, a couple of subtle knots, an asymmetric top fork.
Show it at believable full length; it may extend beyond the pack's outline. Do
**not** shrink it to a stub, and do **not** run it across the top or back of the
pack.

## 5. Rendering style

Flat **Friendly Forest** style: clean linework, rounded joins, `#1E1E1E`
outlines, no shading gradients, no photorealism, no painterly rendering, no raster
texture. Handcrafted and warm — "found and repaired," not manufactured.

---

## 6. Must / must-not checklist

**Must:**
- one backpack, alone, 3/4 view, plain background
- acorn flap with quilted cap texture and a **lengthened nut** to a soft point
- flap length = half the pack height
- **leather top loop** present
- **acorn** cookie cutter (hollow metal), tied to the **top loop**
- **enlarged** oak-leaf patch on the body, fully below the flap
- full-length found-oak walking stick, **side-mounted** on two plain leather straps

**Must not:**
- no character, body, or tail
- no stem on the flap
- no free-hanging medallion; no wooden toggle between straps
- no oak-leaf-shaped cookie cutter (use the acorn shape)
- cookie cutter not tied to the walking stick
- walking stick not across the top/back; not shrunk to a stub
- no text, labels, numbers, callouts, grids, or extra objects
