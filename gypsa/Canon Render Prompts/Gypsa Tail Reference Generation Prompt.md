## Basic fur construction  

Treat each visible fur spike as though it were an individual straight construction line originating at the structural centerline and extending outward into the fur envelope.  

Fur must always flow **from root toward tip**, as though the entire tail had been gently combed from left to right.  

No fur spike may lean backward toward the root.  

Draw fur on both the upper and lower sides of the centerline. Upper and lower fur should both have a positive left-to-right/distal component.  

Through the normal mature portion of the tail:  
- Standard fur-spike length: 160 px  
- Standard fur-spike angle: approximately 45° relative to the centerline  
- Upper fur angles approximately 45° upward and toward the right.  
- Lower fur angles approximately 45° downward and toward the right.  

Fur should have the soft, flowing, slightly irregular appearance of a stylized eastern gray squirrel tail rather than forming a perfectly geometric sawtooth pattern. The quantitative construction rules govern the underlying fur direction and length; subtle organic variation may soften the final silhouette.  

## Root transition: 0–160 px  

Tail fur must **NOT begin at full length at the root.**  

At the ROOT:  
- Fur-spike length begins at approximately 40 px, which is 25% of the standard mature 160 px fur length.  
- Fur already leans distally toward the right.  

During the first **160 px of structural centerline**, smoothly and progressively increase fur-spike length from **40 px to 160 px**.  

There must be no sudden step or abrupt flare. The transition should resemble short body fur gradually becoming the longer fur of an eastern gray squirrel's tail.  

At **160 px from the root**, fur reaches the standard mature length of approximately 160 px.  

## Mature tail region: 160–1400 px  

Maintain approximately the standard **160 px fur-spike length** through this region.  

Fur continues to lean approximately **45° toward the distal/right direction**.  

Keep the apparent thickness of this main portion of the tail reasonably consistent. Controlled organic irregularity is desirable, but do not create major random changes in volume.  

## Visible Taper: 1400–1760 px  

Begin the terminal taper at approximately **1400 px**, or 87.5% of structural centerline length.  

From 1400 px toward the 1760 px VISIBLE TIP:  
1. Progressively reduce the effective outward fur length.  
2. Progressively reduce the fur angle relative to the centerline from approximately 45° toward 0°.  
3. Increasingly direct the terminal fur toward the right.  
4. Progressively narrow the upper and lower silhouette toward the tail's longitudinal axis.  

Do not create a rounded, blunt, bulbous, or clipped terminal end.  

## Structural Tip and Visible Fur Tip  

The orange structural centerline ends at **1600 px**.  

The visible fur does **not** end there.  

Terminal fur continues approximately one standard fur-spike length (160 px) beyond the structural tip.  

Therefore:  
- ROOT = 0 px  
- End of root transition = 160 px  
- Beginning of distal taper = 1400 px  
- STRUCTURAL TIP = 1600 px  
- Approximate visible FUR TIP = 1760 px from the root  

During the final terminal region, fur angles progressively approach **0° relative to the structural centerline**. The final central fur spike/group should lie approximately in line with the centerline and extend distally to create a **soft, pointed or nearly pointed fur tip**.  

## Critical constraints  
- Straight horizontal tail only.  
- Structural centerline exactly 1600 px.  
- Root at left; tip direction to right.  
- Root fur approximately 40 px long.  
- Root-to-mature transition occupies first 160 px.

- Standard neutral fur direction approximately 45° toward the distal tip.  
- **Every fur spike must lean toward the tip, never toward the root.**  
- Distal taper begins at approximately 1400 px.  
- Both fur length and fur angle progressively decrease through the distal taper.  
- Structural centerline stops at 1600 px.  
- Fur extends approximately 160 px beyond the structural tip.  
- Final fur approaches 0° and creates a soft point.  
- Do not introduce expression-dependent fluffiness.  
- Do not add color development, markings, or shading studies yet.
