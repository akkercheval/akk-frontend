# The Tangled Trail — 404

*Website pages: the 404 / "page not found" page*

While the Tangled Trail represents website navigation gone wrong, it shouldn't simply have a silly background. It should be a real location in the Friendly Forest where navigation has gone spectacularly wrong.  This is a place where both travellers and items have unexpectedly found themselves.  Those who've found the place aren't entirely certain how to find it again, though it always seems that they quickly find themselves back to Acorn Crossing.  

The viewer arrives along a neglected, crooked trail from the center foreground. This trail is old, nearly overgrown, and clearly not maintained like other paths through the Friendly Forest.  

This trail continues toward the middle/background of the image and ends abruptly at an old tree.  There's a large, nearly rectangular hole in the trunk at the base and nearly center of what is visible.  It might have once had a door on the opening in its trunk, but the door is long gone.  

There's a long wooden bench sitting beside the tree which is in surprisingly good condition.  It looks like it could have been masterfully carved a long time ago, and yet it also looks remarkably well preserved.  The boards of the bench are perfecly smooth and straight, and there's an acorn carved into the center of the back of the bench.  

There's also a small round opening on the left side of the trunk just above the back of the bench.  It has no frame, door, glass, or constructed trim.  

Aside from the bench, the area is overgrown, and various items of different sizes are strewn around.  

Near that dead end, a second, well-maintained Friendly Forest path branches away to the right of the image and exits the scene. There's a sign on a wooden post at the beginning of the path which is clearly labeled "Acorn Crossing".  The right end of the sign is shaped like an arrow head and points away from the dead end down the path.  This path is well-kept, like other paths through the Friendly Forest.  The Acorn Crossing path begins in about the vertical center of the image.  The old tree is beyond this trail.  The forest is heavily overgrown around the tree, aside from the two trails and a small clearing directly in front of the tree itself.  

**Important Structural Elements**
These items are always present, regardless of the scene:  
- the crooked, neglected trail  
- the well-kept trail leading to Acorn Crossing and its sign  
- the old tree  
- the bench  

**Notes about items near the old tree**
- The objects should vary subtly in apparent age and condition. Some seem recently lost; others look as though the Tangled Trail has had them for quite some time.  

- The misplaced objects should look as though they arrived here through unrelated, perfectly ordinary circumstances. They should not be arranged decoratively, symmetrically, or as a deliberate collection.  

**Website composition**  
Leave a relatively calm area of forest/sky in the upper portion of the image for the 404 message and navigation controls. The old tree, bench, trail junction, sign, and misplaced objects should remain primarily in the middle and lower portions of the composition.  

The openings in the tree should remain natural and irregular enough that the tree does not appear to have a face.  These might have been a window and a door once, but no architectural elements are visible on the tree.  

*Although strange and neglected, the Tangled Trail should never feel sinister, haunted, dangerous, or threatening. Its mystery is whimsical and mildly absurd.*  

## Seasons  
### Summer  
In the summer, even the Tangled Trail sees sunlight.  There are flowering vines, a rose bush in full bloom, wildflowers, and grass growing everywhere.  There's a picnic basket beside the bench.  A clearly recognizable, fully assembled black Bb soprano clarinet with silver keys, shown in side view, with a narrow mouthpiece and reed at one end, a cylindrical black body with silver keywork, and a slightly flared bell at the other end is lying horizontally on the right side of the bench.  The flared bell end of the clarinet is closest to the right end of the bench, and the mouthpiece end lies near the center of the bench.  

There's also a wide-brimmed hat, a ball of yarn, and an old wooden chair.  

### Fall
In the fall, all of the leaves on the trees have turned colors and are starting to fall.  The vines are gone, the rose bush and wildflowers are no longer in bloom.  There's a quilt lying partially folded across the bench.  There's a basket of carrots beside the bench.  There's also a football, a top-bound spiral notebook, a pumpkin-shaped cookie cutter, and an empty wooden bucket.  

### Winter  
In winter, it's snowing.  Everything is covered in snow, even the bench, which is partially covered with a thick crocheted blanket.  The trees are bare of leaves.  Sitting on the bench is a mug and a pair of woolen mittens.  Beside the bench is a snow-covered, crocheted teddy bear.  There's also a wooden crate, three silver bells, and a small stack of firewood.  

### Spring  
In the spring, the Tangled Trail is starting to wake up again.  The trees have small leaf buds.  There are daffodils in bloom around the old tree.  It's raining lightly.  The wooden tub sitting beside the bench is partially filled with water.  There's a towel draped across the back of the bench.  A shovel has been propped up against the tree.  There's also an umbrella, a plate, and a pencil.  

## Keep the chaos organic

The chaos should still be organic. The methodology says personality should emerge rather than be imposed, and the 404 page should follow that too. This isn't random chaos.  It is a perfectly plausible woodland place in which an increasingly ridiculous sequence of small things has gone wrong. That makes the joke much stronger.
