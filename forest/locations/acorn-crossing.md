# Acorn Crossing — Home / Main Entrance

*Website pages: Home (`/`)*

Acorn Crossing is the entrance to the Friendly Forest. The visitor sees a large oak tree standing beside a well-worn path. Over time, residents have added flat stones to the path, and a sign hangs from the oak tree. Off to the right side, the visitor sees rolling hills and a river in the distance.

## Seasons

**Summer**
In the summer, everything is green, full, with small flowers along the path. The sky is mostly clear and bright.

**Fall**
In the fall, the leaves are turning colors and starting to fall. The grass is starting to turn brown. The flowers are gone, and the sky is a bit cloudier.

**Winter**
In the winter, there's snow on the ground. The leaves are gone from the oak trees, though the evergreens are still dark green and dusted with snow. The path has been partially cleared and tamped down, with many of the flat stones visible through the snow.

**Spring**
In the spring, there's a light rain. The grass is starting to turn green, and the leaves are not quite full. There are daffodils and snowdrops growing near the path. The path and stones are visibly wet and a little darker from the rain.
